04 / STUDIO

Qinghua Guan · Founder管清华 · 创始人

FOUNDER / 管清华

After years of working in interior design and soft furnishing, I have come to believe that good design requires both aesthetic sensitivity and a deep understanding of reality.从事室内设计与软装行业多年，我始终相信，好的设计需要审美，也需要对现实的深刻理解。

I enjoy exploring the character of each space and finding the right balance between materials, colors, furnishings, and art. To me, aesthetics is not simply about creating beauty. It is about making thoughtful decisions, where every element has a reason to belong.我喜欢探索不同空间的气质，寻找材质、色彩、家具与艺术之间恰到好处的关系。对我而言，审美不仅是创造美感，更是在众多可能性中做出准确的判断，让每一个选择都有它存在的理由。

Experience has taught me that some of the most important challenges begin after the initial design is complete. Refining details, balancing vision with budget, coordinating resources and craftsmanship, and solving unexpected problems are all essential parts of the creative process.多年的项目实践让我明白，真正的挑战往往发生在设计方案之后。如何深化细节、平衡效果与预算、协调资源与工艺，以及解决实施过程中不断出现的问题，都是设计不可分割的一部分。

I value the journey from an idea to its final realization, especially the subtle details that may go unnoticed but ultimately define the quality of a space.我享受从一个想法到最终呈现的过程，也始终在意那些不容易被看见、却决定作品品质的细节。

My goal is to turn thoughtful ideas into spaces that can truly be experienced.让好的创意不仅停留在想象中，而是真正成为可以被感受的空间。
